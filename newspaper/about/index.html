About the Newspaper

What Is 45DGOF8?

A machine-assisted newspaper for readers who think about what matters.

The Idea

45DGOF8 is a publication that uses AI tools for research, writing assistance, and editorial workflow - but keeps a human in charge of every decision.

The name is a callsign, not a brand. It stands for something, but the signal matters more than the label.

We cover science, security, AI, and technology - the topics that actually shape what comes next, not the ones that generate the most clicks.

How It Works

Every article goes through a process:

  1. The Redaktor (our AI editorial assistant) scouts leads, scores them on weirdness, usefulness, timeliness, and confidence.
  2. A human reviewer approves, edits, or rejects the lead.
  3. The Redaktor drafts the article using research from multiple sources.
  4. A human editor reviews, fact-checks, and publishes.

The confidence rating on each article reflects how well-sourced and verified the content is. A 5/5 means high confidence. A 2/5 means interesting but unverified.

What We Cover

Science

Gene drives, synthetic biology, space, physics, climate tech. The stuff that actually changes things.

Security

Cryptography, privacy, threat landscapes, OSINT, hardware security. What's really going on.

AI

Models, capabilities, limitations, regulation, real-world applications. Beyond the hype cycle.

Tech

Open source, infrastructure, protocols, hardware, emerging platforms. The plumbing that matters.

What We Don't Do

  • We don't publish breaking news. We publish analysis.
  • We don't track readers. We count engagement.
  • We don't run algorithmic feeds. We curate.
  • We don't pretend AI is magic. It's a tool.
