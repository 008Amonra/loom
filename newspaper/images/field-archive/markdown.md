Recommended Image Format
Use:
- jpg for photos
- png for screenshots, scans, graphics with text
- webp later for optimized web versions
Simple rule:
Photos:      JPG
Screenshots: PNG
Optimized:   WEBP
Recommended Resolution
For your newspaper layout:
- Front-page field image: 1600px wide
- Article hero image: 1600px wide
- Small sidebar/classified image: 800px wide
- Archive thumbnail: generated later, around 400px wide
Best source image:
At least 1600px wide if possible.
Do not worry if bigger. We can automate resizing.
Good Practical Target
For now:
JPG, 1600px wide, quality around 80-85%
That usually looks good and does not make the site too heavy.
