About the Machine

About the Machine

This newspaper is written by machines, edited by machines, and published by a human who makes every final decision. Here is exactly how it works.

The Pipeline

Every article goes through a three-agent pipeline. Each agent is a separate AI instance - they don't share context, can't see each other's work, and each adds something different.

Step 1

The Reporter

Researches the topic, chooses a narrative style, and writes the first draft. Reporters are creative - they might channel a fictional detective, a western hero, or a movie character. The voice is always unexpected.

Step 2

The Copy Editor

Polishes the prose, fixes grammar, improves readability. The Copy Editor never changes the voice or adds new facts - they make the existing work shine.

Step 3

The Editor-in-Chief

Writes the headline, assigns the section, rates the article on four dimensions, and decides whether it ships. The Editor-in-Chief is always "Redaktion" - a fixed persona in the German newspaper tradition.

The Scores

Every article is rated on four dimensions, from 1 to 5:

Confidence
How well-sourced and verified is this? A 5 means multiple confirmed sources. A 2 means interesting but unverified.
Weirdness
How strange is this story? A 5 means genuinely bizarre. A 1 means routine.
Usefulness
Does this help you make a decision or understand something? A 5 means directly actionable. A 1 means interesting but tangential.
Timeliness
How time-sensitive is this? A 5 means it matters right now. A 1 means it will be relevant for years.

Living Articles

Some articles are marked as "living" - they update automatically. The newspaper monitors the sources listed in each living article. When a source changes, the article gets a timestamped update appended. Living articles carry a yellow disclaimer badge so you know they're not static.

What's AI, What's Human

AI does
Research, writing, copy editing, scoring, source monitoring, article scheduling, and social distribution.
Human does
Editorial decisions, topic selection, final approval, corrections, business strategy, and every judgment call that matters.

The human is Jace - the publisher, the operator, the one who decides what the newspaper becomes. The machines are tools. Good tools, but tools.

The Philosophy

Most AI-generated content is slop - optimized for clicks, stripped of nuance, designed to vanish from your memory within seconds.

45DGOF8 is different. We cover the signals that matter: science, security, AI, and technology. We write with voice and intention. We publish corrections in a public filing cabinet. We score our own confidence and publish the results.

The name is a callsign, not a brand. It stands for something, but the signal matters more than the label.
