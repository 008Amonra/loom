CatArtCreator

A small GPT-powered prompt companion for turning cat ideas into custom cat artwork.

Visit source

CatArtCreator is a small page for making cat pictures with help from a custom GPT. Bring a cat idea: breed, mood, costume, place, color, joke, tiny drama. The tool helps turn it into an image prompt.

That is the whole appeal. It does not need to explain AI very much. The cat does most of the explaining.

What It Does

  • Describe a cat scene
  • Choose a mood or style
  • Turn the idea into a stronger prompt
  • Open the custom GPT when you want help refining it

Field Note

The best use is specific but not over-controlled. Instead of asking for “a cat,” ask for the situation:

A one-eyed tabby cat in a black convertible, winking like it just escaped a very boring meeting, warm evening road light, cinematic but funny.

Specific scenes usually work better than generic cats.

Try It

  • Open the CatArtCreator page
  • Visit the CatArtCreator GPT
