YT Producer

YouTube Shorts generator - turn images into 60-second videos with AI voiceover, Ken Burns motion, and multi-frame layouts.

Visit source

Why it matters: Makes short-form video content from static images. No video editing skills required.

YT Producer takes a handful of images and text, and produces a YouTube-ready Short. The core engine is make_short.py - a CLI tool that handles frame sequencing, Ken Burns motion, text overlays, and optional Piper TTS voiceover.

What It Does

  • Multi-frame layouts - standard, commentary (text beside image), fullbleed (text over image)
  • Ken Burns motion - subtle zoom/pan on static images for visual interest
  • AI voiceover - Piper TTS reads your script in English or German
  • Zone ratios - control how much screen goes to text vs. image
  • Theme colors - earth, wind, fire, water presets for text backgrounds

Pricing

Free mode adds a small “45dgof8 YT Producer” watermark. Licensed mode removes it: $19/month or $97 lifetime.

Field Note

The tool works best with 3-5 images and short text per frame. Long paragraphs don’t work on a phone screen. Think: headline, one sentence, next image.
