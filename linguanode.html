03 / Services

What the node does.

Built for the messages that get dropped when a service only knows the ten biggest languages.

01

Language detection

Identifies the language and dialect first. When a form is not marked in the source, the packet says so instead of assuming.

02

Faithful translation

Meaning before word order. The source text stays in the packet so you can always check it against the result.

03

Cultural and term notes

Politeness level, regional usage, technical vocabulary. The choices are written down in plain language.

04

Indigenous language packs

Chol, Guarani, Yucatec Maya, Nahuatl, Otomi, Totonac and Zapotec. Community-contributed, with no legal guarantee.

05

Routing you define

Give the node your rules once. Privacy rights to one queue, support requests to another, or leave it unrouted.

06

Local by default

Private and compliance-sensitive text stays on this machine. The demo console above makes no network calls at all.

05 / Fine print

What this is, and is not.

  • Not a certified legal translation service. Nothing here is a sworn or legally valid translation.
  • Community translations. The language packs are contributed by speakers. Accuracy is not guaranteed, and gaps are flagged rather than filled with guesses.
  • Private text stays local. Compliance, health, finance and personal material are handled on this machine, never sent to a cloud model without a separate OK.
  • The console is a display. Sample packets are fixed examples. Other input is queued for the local desk and is not auto-translated in your browser.
